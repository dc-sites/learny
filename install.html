Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Learny APK safe to install?
Yes, absolutely. The APK is officially built and signed by the Learny development team. It contains no malware, ads, or tracking scripts.
What is the difference between APK and PWA?
The APK is a native Android application that works best on Android devices and offers offline capabilities. The PWA (Progressive Web App) works on both iOS and Android via your browser and doesn't require downloading a file from outside the app store.
Will my data sync between the APK and PWA?
Currently, Learny stores your data locally on your device for maximum privacy. If you switch between APK and PWA on the same device and browser, your data will remain intact.
Why am I getting an "Unknown Source" warning?
This is a standard Android security feature for apps not downloaded from the Google Play Store. Since you are downloading the official APK directly from us, it is safe to allow this permission for the Learny installer.
