About Us

Welcome to Learny

L

Your Ultimate Learning Companion

Learny is a comprehensive study platform designed to help students manage their learning journey effectively. From task management to time tracking, from motivation to achievement recognition, we've got everything you need to excel in your studies.

Our Mission

To empower students worldwide with innovative tools and resources that make learning more organized, efficient, and enjoyable. We believe every student deserves access to quality study management tools that help them reach their full potential.

Our Vision

To become the leading educational platform that transforms how students approach learning, making it accessible, engaging, and rewarding for everyone. We envision a world where technology bridges the gap between effort and achievement.

Our Team

Learny was created by a passionate team of educators, developers, and designers who understand the challenges students face. Based in Sri Lanka, we're committed to building tools that make a real difference in students' lives.

Development Team

Our skilled developers work tirelessly to bring you the latest features and ensure a smooth, bug-free experience across all devices.

Design Team

Our designers create beautiful, intuitive interfaces that make learning management a pleasure, not a chore.

Our Values

  • Student-First Approach: Every feature we build is designed with students' needs in mind. We listen to feedback and continuously improve based on real user experiences.
  • Quality Over Quantity: We focus on delivering high-quality tools that actually work, rather than overwhelming you with unnecessary features.
  • Accessibility: Education should be accessible to everyone. That's why we offer a robust free version with premium features available for those who want more.
  • Innovation: We stay at the forefront of educational technology, incorporating AI, gamification, and modern design principles to make learning engaging.
  • Community: We're building more than just an app—we're creating a community of learners who support and motivate each other.
